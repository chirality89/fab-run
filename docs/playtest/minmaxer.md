# Min-maxer

## Where I would quit, and why
Seed 777, step 13 (NMOS ship report). By then I had solved it: PM every tool whose chart says "Shift", play any card with a positive "points less lost" line, never stop in the Q-time corridor, say no to every event that pays cash, and farm the notebook with Replay. Cash and cycle time do nothing at the end ("for reference only"), so two of the three numbers on the ship report don't count. The last third of the NMOS route (gate oxidation, implant, anneal) runs on its own with nothing to press. Once I know the line, there's nothing left to optimise. I'd come back if spending cash or time had a real cost, and if the rooms I can't touch weren't 8+ points of the run.

## Problems
1. **Notebook can be farmed by replaying** — A wrong guess on the ship report prints the right answer ("What did hurt this lot: Keyhole void"). Replay this seed with the same moves gives the same answer, so the second run is a free notebook entry plus its unlocks. It works on a rejected lot too. A do-nothing run takes 30 seconds. I got 3 entries without reading one chart, and the unlocked plating card was the best card I saw (1.8 points). The diagnosis is a lookup table, not detective work. Evidence: docs/playtest/shots/minmaxer/123456-44-s123-22-diagnosed.png then docs/playtest/shots/minmaxer/123456-70-s123-24-leaked-diag.png. Also seed 42, steps 11-12.
2. **Cash and cycle time are worthless, so most "tradeoffs" are fake** — The ship report says cost per die is "for reference only" and doesn't score cycle time. So PM (0 cash, 40 min) is free, buying everything is free, the 500-cash inline metrology did nothing (it acts at the last step) and still cost me nothing. Every event that offers cash for risk is a trap: the hot lot (+150 cash for about 2 points) is what failed my first run. The auditor event is a free PM, and its other option just loses cash. I end every run with 600-1400 cash and nothing to spend it on. Evidence: docs/playtest/shots/minmaxer/123456-43-s123-21-report.png, docs/playtest/shots/minmaxer/777-26-s777-09-auditor.png, seed 777 step 13.
3. **Doing nothing ships, and effort only adds ~4 points** — On 123456, no cards and no stops shipped at 70.3%. My careful first run was rejected at 69.5%, and my best line got 74.1%. The whole skill range is about 4 points, and 1.8 of that is one unlocked card. Seed 42 is the exception: skip the Etch PM and you lose 18.5 points there, versus 8.6 with it. But the Etch chart on 123456 had the same "Shift" label and the PM was only worth about 1.4 points. Evidence: docs/playtest/shots/minmaxer/123456-69-s123-23-donothing-ship.png, docs/playtest/shots/minmaxer/42-68-s42-09-donothing.png.
4. **Rooms run themselves and the biggest losses aren't choices** — Plating, CMP, Metrology, Gate oxidation, Implant and Anneal resolve as soon as I arrive ("Nothing could be played here"), so I never see their forecast. Together they're 15-20 points a run. Etch costs about 9 points and its only card makes it worse. The game says the depth variation was "set before the lot got here", but I was never shown that choice. On seed 42 the etch loss (18.5 points) isn't even one of the diagnosis options. Evidence: docs/playtest/shots/minmaxer/123456-36-s123-17-plating.png, docs/playtest/shots/minmaxer/42-69-s42-10-diag2.png.
5. **Dead cards and traps on sale** — "Strip and re-expose" is in every litho hand and always greyed out ("No step on this route does that"), yet the PM stop sells a 100-cash DOE for it. Thick resist is sold for trench etches it makes worse (cd error x1.3). Ionized PVD seed shows up on a flat pad-oxide step at "0.7 points more lost". The shop has the same 3 items on every seed and both lots, never restocks, and a second visit wastes 10 minutes. Evidence: docs/playtest/shots/minmaxer/123456-09-s123-05-pm1.png, docs/playtest/shots/minmaxer/123456-16-s123-08-litho.png, docs/playtest/shots/minmaxer/123456-25-s123-11-shop2.png.
6. **The drift that hurt was labelled "In control"** — On NMOS seed 777, Deposition cost 2.9 points of tool drift at step 2. The map had no SPC flag on it, the chart said "In control: no rule tripped in the last 12 lots", and there was no PM stop before step 2. That was the failure mode the game then asked me to name. Meanwhile the red SPC flag on seed 42's Wet clean was a one-off excursion that had already recovered, so the map flag cried wolf. Evidence: docs/playtest/shots/minmaxer/777-14-s777-06-pm-depchart.png, docs/playtest/shots/minmaxer/42-01-s42-02-room1.png.
7. **Unpriced costs** — Q-time risk is shown as "+0.040 oxide risk" with no unit. I only learned the rate (0.02 is about 2 points) from the hot-lot event. "Starts a Q-time clock" on Pre-deposition clean never showed up anywhere afterwards. The rinse's "qtime oxide x1.1" never gets a points number. Evidence: docs/playtest/shots/minmaxer/123456-29-s123-13-qtcorridor.png, seed 777 step 5.
8. **Sloppy numbers** — "Not paid: the lot was rejected.." has a double full stop. The yield-by-step table mixes "98%", "77%" and "76%" with one-decimal values. Lot cost (2000 + net spend) is never explained. Evidence: docs/playtest/shots/minmaxer/123456-43-s123-21-report.png, seed 42 step 11.

## Compliment
Every card shows "about X points less lost in this room", and the forecasts landed within 0.1-0.5 of the result, so I could actually price things.

## Ratings (1 = bad, 5 = great)
- Clarity: 3 — Per-card point estimates are great, but cash, cycle time and Q-time risk are never turned into anything that counts.
- Real decisions: 2 — Most hands have one card, most stops have one right answer (PM the shifted tool), and the biggest losses come from rooms I can't touch.
- Wanting another run: 2 — I'd come back to farm the notebook by replay, which isn't the same thing as wanting to play.
