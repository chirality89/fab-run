# Min-maxer

## Where I would quit, and why
Seed 42, step 5 (right after Etch). I had already bought the best items, PM'd the red-flagged etch tool and played the over-etch, and the lot still came out of Etch at 73.4% with ~13 points of fixed losses still ahead and a 70% spec. "I solved everything the game lets me touch and the lot is dead at step 4. There's no lever left." On 777 I went all in (every useful item, DOE, PM) and topped out at 59.5%. Three seeds, nine full runs, zero shipped lots. I'd come back only if 70% were reachable by some line, or if the shop showed numbers I could plan with.

## Problems
1. **The spec can't be reached** — every line I tried ends 47.9–59.5% against a 70% spec. Etch alone takes 16.7–27.5 points every run, and nothing I can buy or play takes away more than ~5 of them. The rooms after etch add another ~11–15 points with nothing to play. My best line beat doing nothing by ~10 points and still missed by 10. When every result is "rejected", my choices don't feel like they matter. Evidence: seed 42 step 13 (do-nothing 47.9% vs best 57.6%), seed 777 step 8 (all in 59.5%), docs/playtest/shots/minmaxer/42-37-report.png.
2. **Spending money only makes the score worse** — lot cost = 2000 + cash spent − cash earned, and cost per good die is the only number that changes. My no-shopping run on 123456 got 5.97 at 53.6% yield. My shopping run got 7.36 at 58.9%. My best yield (777, 59.5%) got the worst score (7.77). Rejected lots are "Not paid" anyway. The rational move is to buy nothing. Evidence: seed 123456 step 25, docs/playtest/shots/minmaxer/777-38-report.png.
3. **The diagnosis feels like a coin flip** — on 123456 etch lost 21.7 points on a red etch chart, I picked "Etch residue" (its text matched the screen word for word), and the answer was "Edge ring", even though inner sites were 53–64 and not "clean". On 777 etch lost 16.7 and CMP 7.4, and the answer was the CMP one. The same seed gave different answers and different choice lists depending on what I did. I can't work out the rule, so the notebook feels like luck. Evidence: docs/playtest/shots/minmaxer/123456-45-diagnose-wrong.png, seed 777 step 8.
4. **Prices with no effects** — the shop and the cards in my hand show cash and minutes but never what an item does. The only numbers ("residue ×0.3 · underlayer attack ×1.5") show up on the DOE screen, for cards I may not hold. I had to replay whole runs to price things. My measurements: copper bath (200) ≈ 4 points, the best buy. DUV resist (140) ≈ 2.4 points. Pad conditioner (350) ≈ 0.9 points. Inline metrology (500) is used in the last room and "does not fix anything". It's a trap. Evidence: docs/playtest/shots/minmaxer/123456-12-shop1.png, seed 123456 step 24.
5. **Reload and seeds make everything replayable** — there's no save. Reload restarts the same seed with identical numbers (room 1 = 99.1% every time). "Replay this seed" sits under every room result. I used it as an undo for a mis-tap and to scout each fork. Junk seeds ("abc", -5, a 20-digit number) quietly turn into a random new seed, which is a free reroll. Evidence: seed 123456 step 6, seed 42 step 4, seed 777 step 9.
6. **Most rooms and stops are empty** — in 9 rooms I held at most one usable card (Etch) plus a greyed "Strip and re-expose" with "Its condition is not met" and no hint which condition. Metrology side stops say "coming in a later update" and still cost 20 min. Events can be skipped for free with the Continue button, despite "Choose one option". One event was "A quiet shift: nothing that matters". Cycle time (133–248 min) never affects anything. Evidence: docs/playtest/shots/minmaxer/123456-03-corridor1.png, docs/playtest/shots/minmaxer/42-34-quiet-event.png.
7. **Red SPC flags don't predict the losses** — the flagged wet clean cost the same 0.9 as an unflagged one. PM on the flagged etch saved ~2–3 of ~20 points. PM on the flagged CMP left it at 7.4, worse than the unflagged CMPs. The audit "serviced" wet clean but its chart still read out of control. I learned to ignore the flags. Evidence: seed 42 steps 2 and 7, seed 777 step 7, docs/playtest/shots/minmaxer/42-20-wetclean-after-audit.png.
8. **All six PM buttons say the same words** — all six read "PM this tool", with the tool name far above the button. I PM'd Litho instead of Etch and only found out inside Etch. Evidence: docs/playtest/shots/minmaxer/42-10-pm.png, seed 42 step 4.
9. **Unexplained costs and missing risk numbers** — 20 cash vanished between plating and CMP on 123456 with no message. The hot lot's "+0.02 risk" has no unit. On 42, CMP cost 9.4 after a hot lot against 6.7 without, and I couldn't tell if the hot lot caused it. Evidence: seed 123456 step 20, seed 42 step 9.

## Compliment
The forecast line ("This room will cost about X points") is honest within 0.2 every time, and it's the only reason I could solve anything.

## Ratings (1 = bad, 5 = great)
- Clarity: 2 — the yield forecast is clear, but item effects, SPC flags and diagnosis answers aren't.
- Real decisions: 1 — one card per run, a shop that only hurts the score, skippable events, and a lot that's dead by step 4 whatever I do.
- Wanting another run: 1 — after three seeds I've solved it and the answer is "rejected"; nothing new unlocks either ("start to count in a later update").
