# Slay the Spire veteran, no fab knowledge

## Where I would quit, and why
Seed 777 (NMOS lot), step 11: the Activation anneal room. I'm at 74.3%, I have no cards, the room shows no forecast before it runs, and it takes me to 69.7%, 0.3 under spec. Lost the run in a room I couldn't act in. "In Spire, when I die I saw the intent coming. Here I watched a progress bar finish." I was already close to quitting on seed 42, step 11, when two cardless rooms in a row (plating, CMP) took me under spec and all I could do was click Continue.

## Problems
1. **Hands of one card, and whole rooms with no hand** — Every room dealt the same fixed hand on every seed and both lot types: one card, sometimes two with one of them unplayable. Plating, CMP, metrology, gate oxidation, implant and anneal had nothing: "Nothing could be played here, so the lot went straight through." Those rooms caused my biggest losses (CMP -6.2, plating -4.4, anneal -4.6). There is no draw, no deck and no build. When I asked "what is the build?" at the end of run 1, the answer was "there isn't one". Evidence: docs/playtest/shots/spire-veteran/123456-30-s123-19-plating.png, docs/playtest/shots/spire-veteran/777-49-s777-15-anneal.png.
2. **Cash and cycle time don't matter, so most choices are free** — The ship report says cost per die is "for reference only" and cycle time has no effect. I ended every run with 1200+ cash unspent. So every event that offers "+150 cash for some risk" is an obvious no (hot lot, cheaper slurry), and every side stop outside the Q-time window costs nothing. You can also skip an event's choice with the Continue button and pay nothing; I tested it on seed 42 and my cash didn't change. Evidence: docs/playtest/shots/spire-veteran/123456-37-s123-22-ship.png, docs/playtest/shots/spire-veteran/42-30-s42-08-event2.png, seed 42 step 6.
3. **Danger isn't readable from the labels** — The map's SPC flags and the green "In control" labels said deposition and wet clean were fine on seed 777. Then deposition took 2.7 points from "the tool's drift" and wet clean took 1.2. The map flag on seed 42 also stayed red on room 6 after I had PM'd that tool, so I paid for a useless PM inside the Q-time window. The Q-time cost shows as "+0.040 oxide risk", a unit I can't convert. It turned out to be about 3-4 points; only a later event says "0.02 risk = about 2.0 points". Evidence: docs/playtest/shots/spire-veteran/777-12-s777-07-pm-charts.png, docs/playtest/shots/spire-veteran/42-25-s42-06-pm-qt.png, docs/playtest/shots/spire-veteran/42-27-s42-07-room6.png.
4. **Trap upgrades and trap shop items** — The DOE (upgrade) screen offered to upgrade "Strip and re-expose" for 100 cash, a card that says "Can't play: no step on this route does that" in every litho room on both lot types. The shop's Inline metrology works only at the final step, so it can't help the run that's in front of me. Thick resist is "deep etch only" and there's no deep etch. Each shop had 2-3 items, the same ones every run, with no restock, and none for plating, CMP, diffusion or implant. Evidence: docs/playtest/shots/spire-veteran/123456-10-s123-07-pm.png, docs/playtest/shots/spire-veteran/123456-12-s123-08-litho.png, docs/playtest/shots/spire-veteran/42-10-s42-03-shop.png.
5. **Items and PM show no payoff number, but cards do** — Cards say "About 0.4 points less lost in this room". That's great, and it's the best thing in the game. Equipment, PM and DOE never say what they're worth. I bought the radial gas-flow trim twice, blind. On seed 777 it caused the edge ring that cost me 3.5 points, and I lost by 0.3. I only found out at the autopsy. Over-etch in the gate etch room showed no number at all. Evidence: docs/playtest/shots/spire-veteran/123456-16-s123-10-shop.png, docs/playtest/shots/spire-veteran/777-53-s777-17-diagnose.png, docs/playtest/shots/spire-veteran/777-43-s777-13-gate-etch.png.
6. **The map is one road** — The rooms come in the same order on every seed. The only routing is picking one side stop per gap. That mattered once: CMP on seed 777 had no PM in the gap right before it. Otherwise "PM the flagged tool, avoid waiting in Q-time, skip the rest" solves it. Evidence: docs/playtest/shots/spire-veteran/123456-00-s123-01-start.png, docs/playtest/shots/spire-veteran/42-00-s42-01-start.png.
7. **The Diagnose quiz isn't about my run** — On seed 42 my biggest avoidable loss was the Q-time oxide at deposition (-7.6), and it wasn't among the four answers. The right answer was a 0.5-per-room background tax I had no way to fight. On seed 123456 the answer was a plating fault in a room where I had no cards. Evidence: docs/playtest/shots/spire-veteran/42-39-s42-12-diagnose.png, docs/playtest/shots/spire-veteran/123456-38-s123-23-diagnose.png.
8. **Pacing has no rhythm** — The spike comes at the same fixed place on every Cu run (etch -9.1, -9.1, -9.1, whatever I did). After it come three cardless rooms that just drain yield. There's no boss and no relief. The first real decision (PM which tool) came about 2 minutes in, which is fine. The last real decision comes around room 6 of 9.

## Compliment
Cards that print "about X points less lost in this room", and that update when a PM changes the tool (over-etch flipped from +0.5 to -0.1), are exactly the readable intent I want.

## Ratings (1 = bad, 5 = great)
- Clarity: 3 — Cards and room forecasts are clear. Item, PM and Q-time costs are in units I can't read, and the green "in control" labels hid real drift.
- Real decisions: 1 — One-card hands, worthless cash, free event skips. The only real calls were "PM or play over-etch" and "don't wait in Q-time".
- Wanting another run: 2 — The notebook unlock (Edge-tuned chuck) is a small hook. But the next run is the same rooms, same hands and same shop, with more cardless rooms to sit through.
