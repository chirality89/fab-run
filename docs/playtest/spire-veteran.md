# Slay the Spire veteran, no fab knowledge

## Where I would quit, and why
Seed 42, step 13 (CMP result, 58.9%). I'd spent both PMs, 800 cash, played my card and taken the "free PM" event, and landed at 58.9%. That's the exact same number as my first run, where I had no idea what I was doing. That's when I realised nothing I chose was going to move me anywhere near 70. I only played seed 777 because I'd been told to. It confirmed it: my most "solved" build got 59.4%. Three different strategies all landed within half a point of each other, and every one got rejected.

## Problems
1. **The spec can't be reached and choices barely move the result.** Three runs with different plans ended at 58.9 / 58.9 / 59.4. A zero-choice replay of seed 42 got 47.9, so my levers are worth about 11 points in total. Etch alone costs 16 to 27 every run, so 70% never looks possible. I expected a hard target that good play can beat; it feels like a fixed loss. Evidence: docs/playtest/shots/spire-veteran/123456-39-s123-20-end.png, docs/playtest/shots/spire-veteran/42-39-s42-07-report.png, docs/playtest/shots/spire-veteran/777-39-s777-07-report.png, seed 42 step 15.
2. **There's almost no hand.** 7 of 9 rooms per run had zero cards. My first card showed up at step 3 and was greyed out with "Can't play: its condition is not met", without saying which condition. That card stayed dead in all three runs. In a deckbuilder I never once had a deck, a draw, a choice between two cards or a card reward. The time budget in every room was left unspent. Evidence: docs/playtest/shots/spire-veteran/123456-01-s123-02-room1.png, docs/playtest/shots/spire-veteran/123456-12-s123-09-litho.png.
3. **The diagnose quiz punishes you for reading the screen.** Seed 123456: Etch caused 21.7 of the losses, the etch chart said "rate below centre 8 in a row", and the "Etch residue" option described exactly that. The game said the answer was "Edge ring (inner sites are clean)", but my inner sites were 57 to 69, all red. Seed 777: CMP was flagged all run, so I picked "Removal-rate drift". The answer was "Dishing" (wide vs narrow lines), and line width appears nowhere on screen. I got 1 of 3 right, and the two misses felt rigged. Evidence: docs/playtest/shots/spire-veteran/123456-41-s123-22-diagnosed.png, docs/playtest/shots/spire-veteran/123456-41-s123-23-report-map.png, docs/playtest/shots/spire-veteran/777-40-s777-08-diagnosed.png.
4. **Items and cards show no numbers when you pick them.** Shop items are one line of prose with no points. I only learned the copper bath is worth about 4 points and the pad conditioner about 1.5 by replaying seeds. The over-etch card's real numbers (residue ×0.3, underlayer ×1.5) only appear on the DOE upgrade screen, never on the card in the room. The forecast changes only after I commit, and there's no undo. Evidence: docs/playtest/shots/spire-veteran/123456-09-s123-08-shop.png, docs/playtest/shots/spire-veteran/42-04-s42-03-pm.png.
5. **The red SPC badge teaches the wrong lesson.** In run 1, flagged Etch hit for 22, so I learned "red = elite, PM it". In run 2 the flagged wet clean cost 0.9, the same as a healthy one. My PM on the flagged step-6 Deposition saved nothing (3.1 PM'd vs 2.9 without it). In run 3 an unflagged, healthy Etch still forecast 21. The badge is a "?" rather than an intent number, and no tool health value is shown, so with two flagged tools and one PM I'm guessing. Evidence: docs/playtest/shots/spire-veteran/42-01-s42-02-wetclean-spc.png, docs/playtest/shots/spire-veteran/777-17-s777-04-etch.png.
6. **The map is one road and the stops have obvious best picks.** All three seeds had the same 9 rooms in the same order. The Metrology stop says "coming in a later update", so it's pure cost and I'd never take it. Every event has a free hidden skip: "Continue" leaves without choosing. Their payouts are cash and cycle time, and neither ever showed a use or a limit. I saw the same two events in all three runs. Evidence: docs/playtest/shots/spire-veteran/123456-03-s123-04-corridor.png, docs/playtest/shots/spire-veteran/42-29-s42-06-event.png, seed 42 step 12.
7. **The rewards and the save don't stick.** The one notebook entry I earned says its unlocks "start to count in a later update". One of them, the endpoint detector, I had already bought in a shop. When the browser closed mid-run, reopening the link restarted the lot from 100% with no resume. Evidence: docs/playtest/shots/spire-veteran/42-40-s42-08-diagnosed.png, seed 42 step 11.

## Compliment
The yield-by-step table on the ship report showed me exactly where I lost the run, and the forecast line ("this room will cost about X") was accurate every time.

## Ratings (1 = bad, 5 = great)
- Clarity: 3 — I can read the health bar, the scrap and spec lines and the room forecast, but no option tells me what it's worth before I commit.
- Real decisions: 1 — About 4 choices a run, mostly blind, and my three different strategies all ended within half a point of each other.
- Wanting another run: 1 — The result feels fixed, there's no build to chase and the unlocks don't count yet.
