# Phone player with 5 minutes

## Where I would quit, and why
Seed 777 (NMOS transistor), step 13–14: at 72.9% with three rooms left, I opened PM / DOE for help and got "No tool on the route ahead can be PM'd here", which still cost me 40 minutes. Then the anneal room opened already finished, took 4.5 points and dropped me under spec. "I didn't lose, the game lost for me, in a room where I had no buttons." I came close earlier too, on seed 123456 step 12: the PM / DOE page put about two screens of "×0.525 vs ×0.568" between me and the one button I wanted.

## Problems
1. **The last rooms play themselves** — Plating, CMP, Metrology (copper), and Gate oxidation, Implant, Anneal, Electrical test (NMOS) open already done with "Nothing could be played here". Those are some of the biggest hits (CMP 7.0, Anneal 4.5, Plating 4.4–5.1). On seed 777 my run was lost in one of them. I expected at least one card or choice in each room. What I got felt like being a passenger. Evidence: docs/playtest/shots/phone-5min/123456-28-s1-19-plating.png, docs/playtest/shots/phone-5min/777-47-s3-09-anneal-below-spec.png.
2. **What I do barely changes the result** — Copper lot: 72.0% on seed 123456 and 72.2% on seed 42, with different PMs, buying or not buying, and different cards. Etch took about 9 points both times, and the result screen said only 0.2 of it was drift, even though I'd just PM'd the tool. I couldn't see where the other 9 points came from or what would touch them. Evidence: docs/playtest/shots/phone-5min/42-20-s2-05-etch-result.png, seed 123456 step 14.
3. **Every room makes me scroll two screens to reach the hand** — Each room opens with yield, a 5x5 wall of numbers, slots and a chart with a paragraph-long key. The card and the Run button are about 1000–1400 px down, and the first screen has no button at all. The PM / DOE page is about 4 screens tall: the DOE number soup comes first, the PM buttons sit under it, and after a PM the Continue button is at the very bottom. Evidence: docs/playtest/shots/phone-5min/123456-01-s1-02-room.png, docs/playtest/shots/phone-5min/123456-16-s1-12-pm.png.
4. **Diagnose answer hidden, and the evidence isn't on screen** — Tapping a guess jumps the page back to the top, so the answer ("Not this one... Keyhole void") is below the fold and I thought nothing had happened. Two of my three answers depended on things I never saw: "wide lines are thin", "seam in the middle of the fill". The one I got right was by matching the word "deposition". The quiz is the best part, and it feels like a trick question. Evidence: docs/playtest/shots/phone-5min/123456-34-s1-24-diagnosed.png, docs/playtest/shots/phone-5min/42-37-s2-08-diagnosed.png.
5. **Side stops that cost you and give nothing** — PM / DOE charges 40 min before showing what's inside. Twice it had nothing useful (all tools in control, then "No tool on the route ahead can be PM'd here"). The shop sold the same three items on two seeds, and once only "Inline metrology — does not fix anything by itself". I ended every run with about 1250–1460 of 1500 cash unspent, so cash never mattered. Evidence: docs/playtest/shots/phone-5min/777-44-s3-07-pm-empty.png, docs/playtest/shots/phone-5min/42-32-s2-07-shop.png.
6. **Same cards, same events, a dead card every time** — My hand was one card in most rooms. "Strip and re-expose" sat in every litho hand, greyed out with "Can't play: ... No step on this route does that". Over three runs I saw only two events (Quality audit and Hot lot), each twice, word for word. By run three I was on autopilot. Evidence: docs/playtest/shots/phone-5min/123456-12-s1-11-litho-hand.png, seed 777 steps 3 and 12.
7. **Numbers I can't weigh** — "+0.040 oxide risk", "+30 min cycle time" and "−100 cash" mean nothing to me, because cycle time and cash never visibly cost me anything. The hot lot event shows that it can be done: "+0.02 risk (about 2.0 points of yield)" was the one cost I understood. Cards with no "points" line left me guessing whether they were good, bad or zero. Evidence: docs/playtest/shots/phone-5min/123456-24-s1-17-qt-corridor.png.
8. **Run-ending buttons next to Continue** — After every room, "Replay this seed" and "New seed" sit right under "Continue to the corridor". In every corridor, "Scrap the lot" is right under "Go straight". One sloppy thumb throws the run away, and there's no confirm I could see. Evidence: docs/playtest/shots/phone-5min/123456-03-s1-04-after.png, docs/playtest/shots/phone-5min/123456-04-s1-05-corridor.png.
9. **NMOS lot is too long for five minutes** — 13 rooms, about 45 taps, about 4.5 minutes even when I knew where to scroll. The copper lot took about 5 minutes the first time and 3.5 once I knew the routine. On the plus side, a mid-run reload put me back ("Resumed seed 42"), only one screen back. Evidence: seed 777 step 15, seed 42 step 3.

## Compliment
The yield bar with the Scrap and Spec ticks, plus "This room will cost about X points", told me the whole goal in one glance at 20 seconds.

## Ratings (1 = bad, 5 = great)
- Clarity: 3 — I knew the goal fast, but where my yield went, and what minutes and cash are worth, stayed hidden.
- Real decisions: 1 — One card per room with the answer printed on it, auto-played rooms, and side stops that do nothing.
- Wanting another run: 2 — The notebook unlock after a right guess gave me a small pull, but runs end within 2 points of each other whatever I do.
