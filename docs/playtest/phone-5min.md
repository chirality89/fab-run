# Phone player with 5 minutes

## Where I would quit, and why
Seed 777, step 4 (the Etch result) — for good. Third run in a row where Etch took 18–22 points no matter what I did: no prep (seed 123456), PM + 600-cash endpoint detector + over-etch card (seed 42), healthy tool + 450-cash chuck + no card (seed 777). Every lot landed at 71–73% with spec at 70% and five rooms to go, i.e. already lost. "I can't find the lever, so why play?" Honestly the first real quit moment came earlier: seed 123456, step 12, when reloading the page wiped three minutes of run back to step 1.

## Problems
1. **Etch kills every run and nothing I do changes it** — 21.7, 18.4 and 21.2 points lost at Etch across three very different setups; every other room is 1–8. I expected that PMing the flagged tool, buying the etch upgrade or skipping the risky card would make a real difference. It moved the number by 3 at most, and nothing on screen says what would. Evidence: docs/playtest/shots/phone-5min/123456-32-s123456-17-etch-result.png, docs/playtest/shots/phone-5min/42-19-s42-05-etch-result.png, docs/playtest/shots/phone-5min/777-16-s777-03-etch-result.png.
2. **Reload loses the whole run** — I reloaded at step 4 (like a phone does when you switch apps) and was back at "ready for step 1", yield 100%, cash 1500. On a phone that's the end of the game for me. Evidence: docs/playtest/shots/phone-5min/123456-13-s123456-12-reload.png (seed 123456, step 12).
3. **The game blamed me for the card it handed me** — the over-etch card was my only playable card, and playing it made the forecast better (25.1 → 21.7, 21.4 → 18.1). Then the ship report said the lot died of "Underlayer attack", i.e. that card. I felt tricked, not taught. Evidence: docs/playtest/shots/phone-5min/42-36-s42-11-diagnose.png (seed 42, step 11).
4. **Most rooms are "scroll to the bottom, press the one button"** — wet clean, deposition, plating, CMP: empty hand ("No practices left in your hand" before I ever had any), and the only button is below the fold on a page twice the phone's height, with no hint it's there. 6 of 9 rooms per run had zero decisions. Evidence: docs/playtest/shots/phone-5min/123456-01-s123456-02-room1.png (no button visible), docs/playtest/shots/phone-5min/123456-01-s123456-03-room1-bottom.png.
5. **The red SPC tag doesn't mean anything I can use** — a red wet clean cost 0.9 points (same as a green one); a green CMP cost 8.2; a green, PM'd or upgraded Etch still cost ~20. I learned to read the red tags and then they didn't predict anything. And the room's cost is only shown once I'm inside the room, too late to go PM. Evidence: seed 42 step 2, seed 42 step 9, seed 777 step 4.
6. **A greyed-out card with no reason** — "Strip and re-expose — Can't play: Its condition is not met" every run in Litho. Which condition? It's the only other card I ever saw. Evidence: docs/playtest/shots/phone-5min/123456-12-s123456-11-litho-hand.png.
7. **A dead option and a landmine button in every corridor** — "Metrology… (coming in a later update)" is offered as a choice every time, and "Scrap the lot" is a full-size button right under the main "Go straight" button. "Replay this seed" / "New seed" sit right under "Continue" after every room. One sloppy thumb and I'm afraid my run is gone. Evidence: docs/playtest/shots/phone-5min/123456-03-s123456-05-corridor.png, docs/playtest/shots/phone-5min/123456-02-s123456-04-after1.png.
8. **PM / DOE screen is four phone screens tall** — DOE section full of "underlayer attack ×1.5 · ×0.5 with etch stop" multipliers first, then six stacked control charts before I find the tool I want. Right idea, too much thumb work. Evidence: docs/playtest/shots/phone-5min/42-04-s42-02-pm.png, docs/playtest/shots/phone-5min/42-04-s42-03-pm-tools.png.
9. **The useful stuff only shows up at the very end** — the yield-by-step table on the ship report is exactly what I needed during the run. And the report says diagnosing earns a "notebook" entry, but I never saw a notebook anywhere. Evidence: seed 42, step 11.

## Compliment
The shop and the events are short, fit on one screen, and say "next used at step N" or the exact tradeoff — the only moments that felt like a game.

## Ratings (1 = bad, 5 = great)
- Clarity: 2 — yield-as-health and the big buttons are obvious, but I never understood what controls the room that decides every run.
- Real decisions: 2 — a few shop/event picks per run, and none of them visibly changed the outcome.
- Wanting another run: 1 — three runs, three dead lots at step 4, and a reload wipes progress; nothing pulls me back.
