## What it means for Fab Run

- **Factory games** with a chip theme treat process steps as flavor. None of the pages mention reentrant flow, queue time or excursion tracing.
- **Puzzle games** about chips work at the logic-gate level. None model process integration.
- **fab24hr** shows people in the industry care, and that realism alone can land as stress, not fun. Fun comes first.
- No game found makes process integration a roguelite. That's the opening.
